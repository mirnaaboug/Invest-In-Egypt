# Master Brief — Egypt Trade & Investment Gateway Platform

## 1. Who it's for
- Client: UK Government — Department for Business and Trade, British Embassy Cairo, and partner teams
- Audiences: UK investors, UK exporters, Egyptian businesses, policymakers
- Goal: a user can decide whether Egypt is the right base for expansion into Africa, the Middle East and Europe, and knows exactly what to do next

## 2. What we're making
- Formats (phased): public website + internal dashboard + interactive map + pitch deck
- Core message: Egypt as the gateway hub for trade and investment into Africa, the Middle East and Europe
- Languages: English + Arabic
- Branding: UK Government style (GOV.UK) with a co-branded option; neutral variant available

## 3. Content modules (all included)
1. Free trade agreements — UK–Egypt, AfCFTA, COMESA, EU–Egypt, GAFTA, QIZ, Agadir
2. Bilateral agreements — investment treaties, double-taxation agreements, MoUs
3. Sector profiles — size, growth, local vs international participation
4. Industrial hubs, roads & infrastructure — SCZone, free zones, ports, new cities, rail
5. Egypt as shipping & logistics hub — Suez Canal, routes, transit times
6. Investor toolkit — documents, licences, registration steps, official links (GAFI, SCZone, etc.)
7. Market gaps & opportunities
8. News feed — investment & infrastructure
9. Foreign policy & trade relations
10. Regional breakdown on interactive map — North, East, West, Southern, Sub-Saharan Africa
11. Country indicators — agriculture, energy, investment appetite, currency rate, population, GDP, trade partners
12. Other modules as they come up

## 4. Data rules
- Sources: World Bank, IMF, UNCTAD, Central Bank of Egypt, CAPMAS, GAFI, gov.uk, plus other official sources
- Freshness: static snapshot at launch, monthly updates, live feeds where possible (e.g. currency, news)
- Every figure shows its source and date
- Internal data/reports to be attached and added as primary sources

## 5. Look & feel
- Tone: authoritative and neutral, with persuasive investment-promotion sections
- Must-haves: mobile-friendly, accessible (WCAG), printable country one-pagers, downloadable PDFs
