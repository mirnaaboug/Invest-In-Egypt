# Build notes — public website prototype (19 Nov 2026)

## Design direction
- Editorial style: warm paper backgrounds, big uppercase serif headlines, one crimson accent with gold for figures.
- Dotted (dot-matrix) maps for the hero, the interactive map, the red call-to-action band and the trade strip.
- White icon shortcut tiles overlapping the hero; dashed figure boxes; faint oversized section words and 01/02/03 numerals.
- Colour-block sector tiles (orange, navy, rose, olive) with white pill buttons; full-width red band with a "Start here" link; light grey footer.
- No photography yet: real photos can replace the dot-map and line-illustration panels once supplied.

## Responsive behaviour
- Below 1180px the header switches to a menu button with a full section list.
- Below 980px: single-column layouts, map legend below the map, stacked tables, "Back to map" button in the country panel.
- Touch: swiping over the map scrolls the page; panning only after zooming in; larger tap targets for hubs, chips and zoom buttons.
- Charts redraw to the container width so labels stay readable.

## Data status
- Egypt and UK–Egypt figures: sourced and dated (DBT factsheet 6 Nov 2026, UNCTAD WIR 2026, IMF WEO Oct 2026, CAPMAS, SCA, SCZone, bank FX 15 Nov 2026).
- Other African country population/GDP: indicative placeholders — replace with World Bank/IMF data before launch.
- Treaty dates (UK–Egypt BIT 1975, DTC 1977) to verify against gov.uk treaty records.

## Not yet done
- PDFs and print; live currency/news feeds; full Arabic body text; newsletter sign-up not connected.
- Labelled "Prototype, not an official government website"; no crown or GOV.UK marks.
